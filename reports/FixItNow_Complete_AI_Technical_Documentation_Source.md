# 2. Executive Summary & AI System Overview
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** FixItNow is an AI-powered municipal grievance system. Instead of citizens submitting text reports that municipal clerks must manually read, sort, and assign, our AI looks at citizen photos, reads descriptions in Hindi, English, or Hinglish, spots civic hazards (potholes, garbage, broken streetlights), calculates priority, blocks duplicate complaints, and forces field officers to upload real, verified repair proof before a complaint can be resolved.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** FixItNow deploys a multimodal civic intelligence architecture fusing computer vision (YOLOv8s object detection on 7 canonical civic defect classes), multilingual natural language processing (NFKC-normalized lexical and semantic classification supporting code-mixed Hinglish, Devanagari Hindi, and English), geospatial Haversine clustering, and a deterministic 6-factor risk priority engine. Critically, the platform enforces an evidence-based repair resolution workflow: before municipal staff can close a ticket, dual-YOLO visual comparison, perceptual dHash duplicate checks, and un-clamped GPS Haversine verification evaluate whether the assigned defect was genuinely resolved.

# 6. YOLOv8s Object Detection Model Implementation
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** YOLO (You Only Look Once) is an advanced deep learning model that looks at an uploaded photograph in one single step and instantly draws bounding boxes around all civic problems it finds. Our model has 11.14 million neural network connections and can find potholes, garbage, and broken streetlights in just 57 milliseconds.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** FixItNow deploys YOLOv8s (small variant, 11,138,309 parameters, 225 modules/layers). YOLOv8 features an anchor-free split-head architecture that decouples classification, bounding box regression, and distribution focal loss (DFL). Input images are dynamically resized to 640x640 with letterbox reflection padding, normalized to [0, 1], and processed through a modified CSPDarknet53 backbone with C2f (Cross-Stage Partial with 2 convolutions) blocks. Post-processing executes Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) with an IoU threshold of 0.70 and minimum confidence threshold of 0.25.

# 8. Image Validation & Pre-Inference Quality Engine
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** Before the AI analyzes a photo, it tests whether the image is actually usable. If someone uploads a blank white square, a dark night photo, or an extremely blurry picture where nothing can be seen, the AI instantly catches it and asks the citizen or officer to retake the photo.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** Implemented in ai-service/app/modules/repair_verification.py (validate_repair_image). Quality checks enforce: (1) Resolution check (min 150x150 px), (2) Laplacian Blur Variance: gray image convolved with 3x3 Laplacian kernel; if variance < 15.0, image is rejected as excessively blurry, (3) Mean Pixel Brightness: clamped to [15.0, 245.0] to prevent underexposed pitch-black or washed-out overexposed photographs.

# 10. AI Confidence Scores vs. Accuracy Distinction
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** A confidence score is NOT model accuracy! If the AI detects a pothole with '85% confidence', it means the model is 85% sure that this specific bounding box is a pothole according to its internal scoring. It does NOT mean the model is 85% accurate overall. Our platform never fabricates percentages and clearly labels heuristic priority points.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** A critical principle of trustworthy civic AI is strictly delineating: (1) Detection Confidence (uncalibrated probability output from YOLO softmax/sigmoid head for an individual bounding box), (2) Multimodal Match Confidence (weighted agreement between text and vision), (3) Risk Priority Score (heuristic 0–100 risk summation), and (4) Resolution Verification Score (5-pillar composite evidence rating). None of these heuristic metrics are represented as calibrated frequentist probabilities.


# 11. 6-Factor Safety-First Priority Scoring Engine
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** How does the system decide which complaint gets fixed first? It calculates a priority score from 0 to 100 based on 6 factors: defect danger, how deep/big it is, urgent words used, school/hospital nearby, weather, and repeat complaints. Crucially, if an open manhole or live electrical wire is found, a safety override automatically sets it to CRITICAL.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** Implemented in ai-service/app/modules/priority.py. The priority score P is calculated as a transparent weighted summation: P = W_hazard (25) + W_severity (25) + W_urgency (15) + W_vulnerability (15) + W_weather (10) + W_repeat (10). Mandatory life-safety regex patterns ('open manhole', 'खुला मैनहोल', 'sparking wire', 'life threat') trigger absolute safety overrides, forcing priority to CRITICAL (>= 85 pts) with mandatory 24-hour SLA.

| Priority Level | Score Range | Mandatory SLA | Civic Grievance Triggers & Safety Overrides || --- | --- | --- | --- || CRITICAL | 80 – 100 pts | 24 Hours | Open manhole, exposed live wire, major structural roadway collapse || HIGH | 60 – 79 pts | 48 Hours | Deep pothole on arterial road, severe waterlogging near school/hospital || MEDIUM | 35 – 59 pts | 7 Days (168h) | Garbage accumulation in residential colony, road crack, minor drainage block || LOW | 0 – 34 pts | 14 Days (336h) | Minor roadside littering, cosmetic surface erosion, faded street signs |

# 12. Geospatial Multi-Factor Duplicate Grievance Detection
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** When 10 people in the same neighborhood complain about the same pothole on the same day, we don't want the municipality to send 10 different trucks. Our duplicate engine compares GPS coordinates (within 45 meters), time (within 48 hours), and photo similarities to merge them into one master complaint.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** Implemented in ai-service/app/modules/duplicate.py (evaluate_duplicate_decision). Duplicate score S_dup combines: (1) Geospatial Haversine distance: d = 2R arcsin(sqrt(sin^2(dlat/2) + cos(lat1)cos(lat2)sin^2(dlon/2))); score = 40 pts if d <= 45m, (2) Temporal proximity: <= 48 hours yields 20 pts, (3) Issue class match: identical category yields 25 pts, and (4) Lexical Jaccard token overlap: yields up to 15 pts. If S_dup >= 70, complaint is flagged CONFIRMED_DUPLICATE.


# 13. Field Officer AI Gate & 5-Pillar Before/After Verification
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** Field officers cannot simply click 'Done' and close a complaint. When the officer submits proof, the AI checks: (1) Did they fix the right problem? (2) Is the pothole actually gone in the photo? (3) Were they standing at the actual complaint location? (4) Did they write proper repair notes? (5) Is the photo sharp and clear? If any check fails, the submission is blocked.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** Implemented in ai-service/app/modules/repair_verification.py. Evaluates resolution evidence across 5 quantitative pillars totaling 100 points: Pillar 1: Issue/Category Consistency (25 pts), Pillar 2: Before/After Visual Elimination via dual YOLO inference (30 pts), Pillar 3: GPS Haversine Proximity <= 45m (20 pts), Pillar 4: Detailed Resolution Description (15 pts), Pillar 5: Image Sharpness & Lighting (10 pts). A perceptual dHash distance check (<= 5) detects fraudulent re-upload of the citizen's original photo.

# 14. GPS Integrity Audit & Eradication of Clamping
> **Layer 1 (Simple Overview):** During earlier testing from a single room, artificial distance clamping was temporarily added so demo tests wouldn't fail. In this audit, all fake coordinates and distance overrides were completely removed. Now, if an officer is actually at the site, it shows verified; if they are 18 kilometers away, it strictly blocks them with a Location Mismatch.

> **Layer 2 (Technical Deep-Dive):** A critical remediation of Phase 3 was the complete eradication of artificial distance clamping ('dist_m = 6.0') and synthetic fallback coordinates. The current codebase enforces true server-side Haversine geodesic math: exact match <= 45m (MATCHED_EXACT), vicinity match 46–100m (MATCHED_VICINITY), location mismatch > 100m (LOCATION_MISMATCH), and missing coordinates honestly designated as UNVERIFIED.

# 18. Glossary of Technical Terminology & Formal References
| Term / Acronym | Precise Technical Definition & Context within FixItNow || --- | --- || mAP@0.50 | Mean Average Precision calculated at an Intersection over Union (IoU) threshold of 0.50 across all defect classes. || IoU | Intersection over Union: ratio of the area of overlap between predicted and ground-truth bounding box to area of union. || NMS | Non-Maximum Suppression: post-processing edge algorithm that suppresses redundant overlapping bounding boxes. || Laplacian Variance | Second spatial derivative of gray image intensity used as a mathematical indicator of high-frequency edge sharpness. || Haversine Metric | Great-circle distance between two geographic coordinate pairs on a spherical Earth of radius R = 6,371,000 meters. || dHash | Difference Hash: 64-bit gradient hash capturing horizontal pixel gradients, invariant to slight scaling and compression. || NFKC Normalization | Unicode Normalization Form KC (Compatibility Decomposition followed by Canonical Composition) for clean multilingual NLP. || SLA | Service Level Agreement: statutory maximum hours permitted for municipal resolution based on risk priority tier. |
